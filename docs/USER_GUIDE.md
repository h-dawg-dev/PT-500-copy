# User Guide (Members)

For members of the TAMU Physical Therapy Society.

## Sign in

1. Open the app: https://pt500.vercel.app/
2. Click **Sign in** and authenticate through the Clerk sign-in page.
3. Your name and a `member` (or `officer`) badge appear in the header.

To sign out, click your name in the header and choose **Sign out**.

## Your home chart

After signing in, the home page shows:

- **Points earned** this term out of the points required (7 by default).
- **Requirements** that are still unmet, including any category shortfalls
  (e.g. 1 professional point, 1 social point).
- **Next event** — the soonest upcoming event.
- **Your points history** — the events an officer has marked you present for,
  each with its point value, and any point adjustments an officer made, with
  the reason. Newest first.

Points reset each term (semester); only the current term is shown.
When no term covers today, the chart says **Current term unavailable** and shows
your all-time check-in history instead of claiming semester eligibility.

## Announcements about your points

When an officer adds or subtracts points for you, a pop-up appears the next time
you open the app. It lists each change since you last closed it: the amount,
category, reason and date.

- Close it with the **Close** button or the **Esc** key. It will not appear again
  for those changes.
- If nothing changed, no pop-up appears.
- You only ever see changes to your own points.
- Every change also stays in **Your points history** on your home page.

## My information

Click your name in the header and choose **View my information** to see your
intake form answers, then press **Update my information** at the bottom of the
page to change them. If you have not submitted the intake form yet, the page
links to it instead.

- Only your name and email are required.
- If something is not valid, the form says what to fix and nothing is saved until
  every field is valid.
- Your sign-in account, role, term, and points are managed separately.

## Event board

Open **Events** in the header (or `/events`).

- Press `/` (or click the search box) to search by event title, room, notes,
  attendee name or email, term, or date (including `2026-09-22`). Typing filters
  the events already shown; press **Search** to search the selected list.
- Use **Upcoming / Past / All** to filter the list.
- Click an event to see its details, then **RSVP** so officers know to expect
  you. You can cancel your RSVP the same way.

Being marked present at an event is what awards points; an RSVP is a heads-up,
not attendance.

## Intake form

After you sign in, a reminder at the top of the page links to a short form (name,
email, classification, expected graduation, major, transfer/first-generation
status, T-shirt size, email opt-in) until you submit it. Name and email are
required; demographic questions are optional and can be skipped. Click a selected
choice again to clear it. Errors keep your other answers so you can correct the
form without starting over.

You only submit the form once. To change your answers afterward, click your name
in the header, choose **View my information**, and press **Update my
information**. Responses are visible to officers in **Member intake forms** and on
your member record.
