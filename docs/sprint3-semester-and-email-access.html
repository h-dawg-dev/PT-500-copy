Semester rollover

Points are calculated from attendance and manual adjustments for the current term. The term changes automatically at midnight in America/Chicago on its configured start date. End dates include the entire local day. UTC timestamps are converted to Central time before choosing a term, including daylight-saving transitions.

For example, a member with seven Fall points begins Spring with zero Spring points. The Fall attendance, adjustments, reasons, and reports remain available. Spring events and corrections count only toward Spring. Repeated reads and event reassignment do not add reset records or duplicate credit.

  1. Before each semester, an officer opens Terms and semesters and creates a term with the approved dates and requirements. Terms cannot overlap.
  2. At its start date, the new term becomes current automatically. No destructive reset, officer reset action, or scheduled job is required.
  3. On a member record, select a History period to inspect prior attendance and adjustment reasons, or choose All time. The correction form continues to apply to the current term.
  4. Use Event import and CSV reports to download members/points and attendance by term or all time. Period/Term columns identify the semester.

An adjustment form opened in one term and submitted after the boundary is rejected with a request to review the new term. The server assigns the current term to corrections and ignores a submitted adjustment term ID. Officers retain attendance records but earn no member points and cannot receive manual point adjustments.

If no term covers today, the app shows Current term unavailable and permits no current-term adjustments. Administrators must supply the next approved date range. Existing reference terms are examples already in the repository, not a new claim of customer-approved transition dates.

Email access policy

The server requires Clerk's verified primary email to be either an exact @tamu.edu address or ptsocietytamu@gmail.com. Surrounding whitespace and case are normalized. Other Gmail addresses, Gmail plus aliases, TAMU subdomains, suffix/lookalike domains, missing primary emails, and unverified emails are rejected.

The rule applies to every authenticated request, including existing sessions and direct officer requests. A denial returns HTTP 403 with a generic explanation and sign-out control, without changing stored member data. Existing valid TAMU members and officers keep their stored permissions.

The organization Gmail account always has member-level access. Its address cannot gain officer rights through OFFICER_EMAILS, the admin promotion endpoint, or the promotion interface. A legacy officer role on that account is demoted on its next verified sign-in. Other allowed TAMU officers retain their officer role.

The former optional, new-signups-only switch is replaced by this mandatory access policy. The migration enables the stored setting and changes its default to true. Attempts to disable it fail validation; authentication also enforces the policy independently of that stored flag. This section supersedes older documentation describing unrestricted existing accounts or an optional signup switch.

One place to update member information

The account menu and new-member check-in now lead to one editable Your information page. Member details and optional intake answers share one Save your information button. Saving keeps the page editable, so members can change either section without returning to a separate submission screen. Name and email remain supplied by the signed-in account.

Both sections save in one database transaction. If either save fails, neither section changes, and the entered values remain on the page with an error explanation. Existing and legacy intake responses are updated rather than duplicated. Old questionnaire endpoints remain compatible with previously opened forms.
