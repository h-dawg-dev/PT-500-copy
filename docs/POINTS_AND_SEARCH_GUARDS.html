Attendance privacy and point corrections

Searching events

Members can search event titles, rooms, notes, terms, and dates. Other attendees' names and emails are excluded from member search data and search matching. Officers can still search attendees by name or email in either event board.

Changing recorded credit

Reducing an event's points, changing its category or semester, deleting the event, or removing attendance is rejected if it would take a member's category points below zero in the original semester. The change is not saved. The message identifies the affected member and category.

For example, 3 attendance points minus a 2-point correction leaves 1 point. Removing that attendance would leave −2, so it is blocked. First record a +2 adjustment with a reason explaining the reversal, then remove the attendance. The final balance is zero and both adjustments remain in the history.

Changes that leave exactly zero are allowed. Officers remain outside the member points system. These safeguards prevent new invalid credit removals; they do not rewrite existing balances or adjustment history.
